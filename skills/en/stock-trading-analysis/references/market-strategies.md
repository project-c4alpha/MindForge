# Market-Differentiated Strategies Details

## A-Share Market - Price-Driven Strategy

### Core Philosophy

Technical analysis and price targets as primary, de-emphasize long-term value investing logic. A-share market is dominated by swing trading, with high opportunity cost for long-term holding.

### Trading Style

Swing trading, price target management, disciplined entry/exit

### Analysis Weight

Technical 70% + Short-term News 20% + Market Sentiment 10%

### Key Elements

- Clear price targets (target price, take-profit price, stop-loss price)
- Strictly execute entry/exit discipline when expected prices are reached
- News only as short-term catalyst reference, not for long-term holding
- Emphasize capital flow, major player behavior, theme popularity
- Focus on short-term policy impact, not long-term value

### A-Share Special Focus

| Indicator | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Limit Up/Down | Number of limit-ups, risk of limit-up opening, limit-down risk |
| Turnover Rate | Activity level judgment, too high may indicate top |
| Dragon Tiger List | Hot money/institutional seats |
| Volume Ratio | Volume change, assess capital activity |
| Consecutive Limit-ups | Days of consecutive limit-ups, assess theme strength |
| Northbound Funds | Foreign capital inflow/outflow |
| Main Capital | 5-day net inflow/outflow |

### A-Share Trading Discipline

| Stage | Execution Discipline |
|-------|---------------------|
| Entry | Enter in batches after technical signal confirmation |
| Hold | Monitor volume-price coordination, don't change plan due to news |
| Reduce | **Must execute** when reaching target price, regardless of news |
| Stop Loss | **Must exit** when hitting stop loss, don't hold for "long-term value" |

### A-Share Special Risks

- **Target Not Achieved Risk**: May not reach expected target price
- **Quick Spike and Fall Risk**: Theme stocks prone to rapid rise then fall
- **Liquidity Risk**: Small caps may have difficulty selling in time
- **Policy Sudden Change Risk**: Regulatory policies may suddenly tighten
- **T+1 Risk**: Cannot sell on the same day of purchase

### A-Share Not Recommended

- Holding A-share individual stocks based on long-term value investing logic
- Ignoring technical signals and persisting in holding
- "Value investing" without stop loss
- Chasing high to buy theme stocks that have risen significantly

## US Stock Market - Value and Growth Balance

### Core Philosophy

Combine fundamental analysis with technical analysis, emphasize long-term value. US stock market is relatively mature, suitable for medium to long-term holding of quality companies.

### Trading Style

Value investing, growth investing, long-term holding

### Analysis Weight

Fundamentals 50% + Technical 30% + Macro 20%

### Key Elements

- In-depth fundamental and business model analysis
- Emphasize earnings season impact
- Consider Federal Reserve policy
- Focus on long-term growth potential
- Suitable for medium to long-term holding

### US Stock Analysis Focus

| Level | Analysis Content |
|-------|-----------------|
| Company Level | Earnings, performance expectations, business model, management |
| Industry Level | Industry trends, competitive landscape, technological innovation |
| Macro Level | Fed policy, economic data, geopolitics |

### US Stock Valuation Metrics

- **PE/PB/PS**: Comparison with industry
- **PEG**: Price/earnings to growth ratio
- **DCF**: Discounted cash flow (if data available)
- **Institutional Ratings**: Buy/Hold/Sell statistics

### US Stock Time Dimension Recommendations

| Timeframe | Analysis Focus |
|-----------|---------------|
| Short-term (1-4 weeks) | Technical + Catalyst |
| Medium-term (1-6 months) | Fundamentals + Trend |
| Long-term (6+ months) | Value + Growth |

## Cryptocurrency Market - Trend and Sentiment Driven

### Core Philosophy

Technical analysis primary, high focus on market sentiment and capital flow. Cryptocurrency market is highly volatile, requiring strict risk control.

### Trading Style

Trend trading, quick entry/exit, strict stop-loss

### Analysis Weight

Technical 60% + Market Sentiment 25% + On-chain Data 15%

### Key Elements

- 24/7 trading characteristics
- High volatility, strict stop-loss
- Focus on market sentiment and on-chain data
- Highly sensitive to regulatory policies
- Watch Bitcoin correlation
- Quick in/out, not recommended for heavy positions

### Cryptocurrency Analysis Focus

| Dimension | Specific Content |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Market Sentiment | Fear & Greed Index, social media heat |
| On-chain Data | Holding address distribution, exchange inflow/outflow, whale movements |
| Technical Progress | Upgrades, forks, ecosystem development |
| Regulatory Dynamics | Regulatory policy changes in various countries |

### Cryptocurrency Risk Control

| Risk Type | Response Strategy |
|-----------|------------------|
| Volatility Risk | Strict stop-loss, no more than 5-10% of total position |
| Liquidity Risk | Choose mainstream coins, avoid small caps |
| Regulatory Risk | Monitor policy trends, adjust timely |
| Technical Risk | Choose mature projects, avoid new projects |

### Cryptocurrency Not Recommended

- Heavy position in single coin
- Holding without stop-loss
- Chasing high to buy coins that have risen significantly
- Ignoring regulatory policy changes
